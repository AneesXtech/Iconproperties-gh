Privacy Policy

ICON Estate & Property Managers ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data when you visit our website or interact with our real estate services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a consultation, or submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Property preferences, location requirements, and budget details
  • Enquiry messages or other information you provide through forms
  • Any other information voluntarily submitted to us

We may also collect non-personal data automatically, such as:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • Referral website URLs and usage patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide requested real estate services
  • Connect property buyers, sellers, and tenants with relevant opportunities
  • Send important updates or communications related to our services
  • Improve our website, offerings, and client experience

We will never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

3. Data Storage And Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Your data is stored securely on protected servers and is only accessible by authorized personnel who require access to perform their duties.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only with trusted service providers who assist us in delivering our services, such as:

  • Email and communication service providers
  • IT and website hosting providers

We ensure all partners comply with strict data protection standards and use your information only for the specific purposes authorized by ICON Estate.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate data
  • Request the deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of any marketing communications
